# Feature Availability FAQ - Pick the Game Pools

### Will you offer college football pools?
**Tags:** college-football, leagues

Yes. College football pools are supported.

Commissioners can create pools for NFL or College Football, and college teams are grouped by conference in the app.

### Are SMS reminders available?
**Tags:** roadmap, notifications, sms, push

Yes. Optional SMS pick reminders are available. Open **Account Settings**, choose **Text** under **Pick reminders** (or **Set up text reminders** if no phone is saved), complete the consent form, and verify your number with the six-digit code. Saving a phone number alone does not enroll you.

You can also reply to Coach AI by text to ask pool questions or submit picks. Email and app push reminders remain separate choices. Read the [SMS reminders release announcement](https://www.pickthegame.com/news/sms-text-reminders/) for an overview.
