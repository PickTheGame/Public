# Tiebreakers FAQ — Pick the Game Pools

### How is the tiebreaker calculated?
**Tags:** tiebreaker, scoring, rules

The tiebreaker is based on **closest to the actual points scored**, regardless of whether the guess is over or under.  
The difference from the actual score is what matters.

Example:

| Guess | Actual | Difference |
|-------------|--------------|-----------|
| 40 | 44 | 4 | 
| 48 | 44 | 4 | 

Both are equally close.

### Can tiebreaker points be hidden until the game starts?
**Tags:** tiebreaker, visibility, picks

Yes. Tiebreaker guesses remain hidden in the Matrix while any game pick is still editable under the pool's submission schedule. They become visible after all picks are locked.

### Can everyone in my pool see tiebreaker results in standings?
**Tags:** tiebreaker, standings, visibility

Yes. Commissioners can enable tiebreaker columns to be visible to all members in the standings view.
