# Platform & Technical FAQ - Pick the Game Pools

### How can I download or install the Pick the Game app?
**Tags:** download, downloads, install, installation, mobile app, ios, iphone, ipad, apple, app store, android, google play

Download the native Pick the Game app from your device's store:

- **iPhone or iPad:** [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pick-the-game/id6741455121).
- **Android:** [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pickthegame.core).

Choose the link for your device. You can sign in with your existing Pick the Game account.

### Is there a mobile app?
**Tags:** mobile app, ios, iphone, ipad, android

Yes. Pick the Game has native apps for iPhone, iPad, and Android. See **How can I download or install the Pick the Game app?** above for the store links. The responsive web app also works in mobile browsers.

### Is the platform mobile-friendly?
**Tags:** mobile, responsive, usability

Yes. The platform is built mobile-first and works on phones, tablets, and desktops.

### Can I receive email or SMS reminders?
**Tags:** notifications, email, sms, push

Yes. You can manage notification preferences in **Account Settings**.

Available options include pick-submission confirmation emails, pick due reminders by email, app push notification, and optional SMS, and pool-message push notifications. Commissioners can also choose to receive emailed copies of player pick submissions.

To enable SMS, complete the consent and phone-verification flow in Account Settings. Text is currently available for pick reminders, not every notification category. See the [SMS release announcement](https://www.pickthegame.com/news/sms-text-reminders/) for the main features.

### Why didn't I receive a pick reminder email?
**Tags:** email issues, notifications, support

First, confirm that pick due notifications are enabled in **Account Settings**.

Then check your spam or junk folder. If you're still not receiving emails, contact support for assistance.

### Where can I see past notifications?
**Tags:** notifications, history, unread, account

Open **Account Settings** and select **View notification history**. The Notifications page shows messages sent to you, lets you open the related pool when available, and lets you mark one or all notifications as read.


### How do I set up SMS text reminders?
**Tags:** sms, notifications, phone, verification, account

Sign in and open **Account Settings**. If no phone is saved, choose **Set up text reminders**. Otherwise, open **Pick reminders** and choose **Text**. Enter your mobile number, consent to SMS, accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and enter the six-digit code sent to your phone.

Text reminders stay off until verification succeeds. Look for a welcome text from Coach AI at **+1 (866) 486-7901**. A saved phone number alone is not an SMS enrollment. Email and push preferences are separate.

### Can I submit picks by replying to a text?
**Tags:** sms, coach-ai, picks, deadlines

Yes. Once enrolled in SMS, reply to Coach AI to ask pool questions or give a pick instruction, such as "Pick all favorites in my office pool." Include the pool name when needed. Coach AI may ask for a tiebreaker or other missing information before it can finish.

Pool permissions, eligible teams, and game locks still apply. Check the app to confirm the saved selections. Text replies are concise; charts and interactive forms are available in the app. Learn more in [Meet Coach AI](https://www.pickthegame.com/news/meet-coach-ai/).

### Why did my SMS verification code not arrive?
**Tags:** sms, verification, phone, troubleshooting

Check the number in the enrollment form and your phone's signal and blocked-message settings. Codes expire after 10 minutes. You can request a new code after the one-minute resend cooldown; use the newest code. Requests are limited to five sends per hour per account or phone.

If you previously replied STOP, text **START** to **+1 (866) 486-7901** before trying enrollment again. Contact **admin@pickthegame.com** if the problem continues. SMS delivery can be delayed, and text reminders stay off until verification is complete.

### How do I stop SMS reminders or get help?
**Tags:** sms, notifications, stop, help, privacy

Turn text reminders off in **Account Settings**, or reply **STOP** to opt out of SMS. Reply **HELP** for help, or email **admin@pickthegame.com**. SMS choices are separate from email and push preferences. To enroll again after replying STOP, first text **START** to **+1 (866) 486-7901**, then complete enrollment in Account Settings.

Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Read the [Terms of Service](https://www.pickthegame.com/terms-of-service.html) and [Privacy Policy](https://www.pickthegame.com/privacy-policy.html) for SMS program and mobile-information details.

### What happens if I change my phone number?
**Tags:** sms, phone, verification, account

Changing or removing your saved phone number revokes its SMS enrollment and turns off text reminders. To receive texts at a new number, complete consent and phone verification again in **Account Settings**. Saving the same number leaves the existing enrollment intact.

### Does a text reminder extend my pick deadline?
**Tags:** sms, picks, deadlines, notifications

No. An SMS reminder does not change your pool's deadline or reopen locked games. Text messages can be delayed or fail to arrive. Check your pool in the app and confirm your picks are saved before the deadline.
