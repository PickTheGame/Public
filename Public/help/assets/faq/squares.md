# Super Bowl Squares FAQ — Pick the Game Pools

### When are square numbers assigned?
**Tags:** squares, superbowl, assignment, rules

If **Use Random Numbers** is enabled, the row and column numbers are assigned when the grid fills or the associated game starts, whichever happens first.

If random numbers are disabled, the grid uses fixed number order.

### What Squares options can a commissioner configure?
**Tags:** squares, settings, grid, scoring

Commissioners can configure:

- A standard 10x10 grid or a smaller 5x5 grid
- The maximum number of squares one player may select
- Random or fixed row and column numbers
- Whether random numbers reshuffle for each quarter
- Winners at the final score only, at halftime and the final, or after every quarter

Squares settings cannot be changed after the associated game has started.
