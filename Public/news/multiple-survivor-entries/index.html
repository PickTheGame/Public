More flexibility for Survivor pools

Some groups love the pressure of one entry and one mistake ending the run. Other groups want a larger field, more strategy, or a way for players to manage more than one path through the season.

With multiple Survivor entries, PickTheGame now supports both styles. Commissioners can decide whether every player gets a single entry or whether players receive extra entries when the pool is created or configured.

What commissioners can control

  • Run single-entry Survivor pools: keep the traditional format where each player has one pick path.
  • Allow extra entries: set how many additional Survivor entries each player gets.
  • Match your group's style: keep things simple for casual players or add more room for strategy in competitive pools.

Why multi-entry Survivor can be useful

Multiple entries make it easier to run larger, more active Survivor pools without asking commissioners to manage duplicate accounts or manual side tracking. Players can take different team paths, recover from one eliminated entry while another stays alive, and stay more engaged deeper into the season.

Built for commissioner choice

This update does not force every Survivor pool to become multi-entry. It simply gives commissioners the choice. If your group prefers the classic one-and-done format, keep it single-entry. If your group wants more chances, more strategy, and a bigger contest, enable extra entries.
